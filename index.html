How a floor gets built

  1. Strategy and space planning

    Headcount, ways of working and growth, set out on the plate before a wall goes up.

  2. Design

    Rooms, routes and neighbourhoods drawn to the brief.

  3. MEP and networking

    Air, power and data planned into the ceiling void, not added later.

  4. Build

    Ceiling, lighting and finishes built by the same team that drew them.

  5. Furniture

    Settings chosen for how each team actually works.

  6. Commissioning

    Every system tested and signed off before people arrive.

  7. Handover

    One Client Success Officer, from kickoff to the keys.

Six ways in, one contract

Design and Build

  • Design and Build

    One team, from first drawing to handover. Strategy and space planning through MEP and networking design to build, furniture and commissioning, with one Client Success Officer from kickoff to handover.

    45 days standard turnaround under ideal conditions

  • Design Consultancy

    Workspaces that command growth. Design thinking combined with a regional reading of how each market works, turned into a workplace that carries the brand across locations.

  • Workplace Strategy

    The evidence before the drawing. Change management after mergers, utilisation studies, hybrid patterns, and the safety and space questions boards now ask first.

    186+ workplace projects delivered

  • WELL Consultation

    Health designed in, then certified. In-house WELL Accredited Professionals build the WELL standard into the design and stay from registration to the rating.

  • WELL Health-Safety Rating

    A floor people will come back to. Wellbeing policy, stakeholder engagement, air and water quality, and the documentation behind each claim, taken end to end.

  • Sustainability Consultancy

    Net zero made an engineering problem. Green building certification, life cycle assessment, embodied carbon tracking, and the energy, water and waste programmes that follow occupancy.
