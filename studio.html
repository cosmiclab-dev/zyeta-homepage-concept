Why we work differently

Six standards Zyeta holds every project to.

  1. Thought leadership

    Zyeta tracks global trends in workplace excellence, workforce dynamics and environmental practice, and brings them into every project.

  2. Technology investment

    BIM, cloud-enabled renders and virtual reality make a floor visible before it is built, and cut cycle time and cost. Zyeta was the first firm of its kind in India to use them.

  3. Integrated capabilities

    One source runs the whole project, MEP and networking design included, so there is no vendor finger-pointing.

  4. Safety commitment

    Safety training is built into every project, from final design through build-out.

  5. Sustainability mandate

    Less water and energy, recycled materials, and suppliers held to the same standard.

  6. People

    Architects, engineers and designers, trained continuously on new technology.
